## A0

Grammar sequence: Recognise je/tu in memorised chunks → Distinguish a question from a statement → Use un/une as part of a familiar noun phrase.

Contexts: alphabet and spelling, sound discrimination, greetings and politeness, objects and numbers, classroom repair, typing and recording.

- Reading: Identify a familiar word or instruction with explicit support.
- Listening: Recognise a very short model with repeats and visual/text support.
- Writing: Copy accurately, then combine two modelled lines.
- Speaking: Say an invented name and a memorised polite request; ask for repetition.

## A1

Grammar sequence: Present être/avoir and regular verbs → Common irregular present verbs → Articles, gender and adjective agreement → Questions and negation → Possessives and demonstratives → Quantity and partitives → Place/time prepositions and contractions → Imperative and near future.

Contexts: identity and family, routine and time, food and money, transport and directions, study and basic work, housing, routine health appointment, simple forms and messages.

- Reading: Extract who, where, when and price from a short practical text.
- Listening: Follow a slow clear exchange and check one detail.
- Writing: Write a short intelligible message with day, time and purpose.
- Speaking: Complete a predictable exchange with support and clarification.
