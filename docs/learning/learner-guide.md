# Learner Guide

## Start a lesson

1. Sign in and open **French courses** from the dashboard.
2. Select any available level to browse it. Browsing does not change the level saved to your account.
3. Open a course and choose **Start lesson**.
4. Read the lesson notes, vocabulary, grammar, and dialogue, then answer each exercise.

Lesson answers are checked by the server. After submitting, the page displays the result and explanation. Your session and progress are saved to your account.

## Resume or review progress

Use the dashboard's active lesson list to resume an unfinished session. The **Progress** page shows per-lesson status, scores, and completion history. Completing a lesson requires answering every exercise. Leaving an unfinished session does not erase it; use **Save and exit** and resume it later. A completed lesson can be taken again, and new results do not replace the historical session.

Progress stays attached to each lesson when you change your current level in the dashboard or Settings. You may browse and revisit lessons from earlier levels without changing your saved current level.

## Scores

Exercise scores describe your answers for that lesson only. They are not a formal measurement or certification of French proficiency. Correct answers and explanations are shown after an answer has been submitted.

## Draft content

Draft lessons are not available to ordinary learners. A development preview, when explicitly enabled for an authenticated administrator, is labeled as unreviewed and must not be treated as approved learner content.
