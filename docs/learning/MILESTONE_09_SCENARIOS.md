# Milestone 09 conversation scenarios

Choose **AI Tutor → Conversation practice** and start text or speaking.
The catalogue shows practice at the account's selected level. Change the
saved level in Settings to choose scenarios at another level. Difficulty
controls in an existing transcript change the wording within its starting
level. The opening and target are practice guidance.

| Scenario | Level | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introducing yourself | A1 | Give a fictional name and ask one question |
| Ordering at a boulangerie | A1 | Order politely and close the exchange |
| Shopping at a supermarket | A2 | Locate an item and confirm a quantity |
| Making an appointment | A2 | Propose a date and confirm the time |
| Asking for directions | A2 | Ask for directions and confirm a step |
| Ordering at a restaurant | A2 | Order and ask for the bill |
| Using public transport | A2 | Ask for a ticket and confirm the stop |
| Visiting a pharmacy | A2 | Ask a fictional service question |
| Talking to a doctor | B1 | Practice describing a fictional symptom and its duration |
| Job interview practice | B2 | Describe fictional experience and ask a relevant question |

Earlier scenarios remain available, including the advanced C1 and C2
practice prompts. The ten situations have original French openings and
at least one bilingual vocabulary example. Speaking uses the existing
recorder, transcription confirmation and playback. Open the shared tutor
transcript from Speak to adjust the coach or finish the conversation.

Pharmacy and doctor use bounded authored dialogue for language practice.
The learner may type freely. These two situations use a short fixed sequence
of fictional prompts and do not generate clinical responses.
